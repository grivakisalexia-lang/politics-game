# Global South Leadership Simulator

Lead a fictional Global South nation through 24 turns of Reigns-inspired decision
making. Balance public support, fiscal space, sovereignty, and environmental
resilience while confronting the structural dependencies that shape many Global
South economies.

## Gameplay

* Each turn you face a narrative event card with two options.
* Choose an option by entering `1` or `2`. Type `c` to reread the context.
* Your decisions adjust four resource meters. Letting any meter hit `0` or `100`
  ends your mandate early.
* Survive for 24 turns to complete the full mandate and review a summary of your
  choices and their consequences.

The deck of events highlights themes such as debt dependence, climate finance
conditionality, digital extractivism, and South-South solidarity.

## Running the game

```bash
python global_south_simulator.py
```

The experience runs entirely in the terminal and requires no additional
dependencies beyond Python 3.8+.
